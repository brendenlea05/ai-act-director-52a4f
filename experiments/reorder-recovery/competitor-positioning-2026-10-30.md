# Distributor reorder recovery — competitor reality check (2026-10-30)

## Direct incumbent, not an empty market

- Proton.ai's Pronto explicitly ranks slipping distributor customers, draws on ERP/CRM transaction history, drafts follow-ups, and integrates with the Proton suite: https://www.proton.ai/pronto . The same vendor markets reorder recommendations and at-risk account identification: https://www.proton.ai/ .
- Its broader distributor cloud CRM/PIM/ecommerce/quote-entry positioning was announced March 17, 2026: https://www.naw.org/mdm/news/proton-ai-launches-unified-industry-cloud-platform-for-distributors/ .
- Order/quote automation became generally available in July 2026, so "AI distributor follow-up" alone is NOT differentiated: https://www.mdm.com/news/breaking-news-in-wholesale-distribution/proton-ai-launches-automated-order-quote-entry-system/ .

## Testable *hypothesis*, not a proven advantage

Focus ONLY on small specialty wholesalers without integrated ERP/CRM or budgets for full-stack tools. Offer a bounded **offline CSV audit** of overdue reorder candidates, explain each flag and require human review of drafts. No claims of AI/ML, integrations, customer uplift, autonomous emails, data security certification, or guaranteed recoveries. Customer order histories must be opt-in and anonymized where feasible; synthetic data otherwise.

## Interview questions / go-no-go

1. How are repeat purchases tracked today: QuickBooks export, spreadsheets, CRM, ERP, or memory?
2. How many monthly repeat buyers and which reorder cycles are predictable vs seasonal/project-based?
3. What percentage of flagged customers would be false positives because of inventory, seasonality, or contract timing?
4. Would a one-time CSV pilot solve a real problem; what specific outcome would justify a paid monthly service?

**Go/no-go:** No subscription build until an independent distributor confirms workflow pain and willingness to pay. $500–$1,000/month is an unvalidated hypothesis. Do not chase already-integrated Proton users with an inferior clone.
