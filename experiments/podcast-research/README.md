# Public podcast guest-fit research sample (5 records)

**Demonstration only; not a client deliverable or a booked appearance.** Research date: 2026-10-29. These five public guest-application pages illustrate the source-linked format for a potential paid podcast research/QA service.

## Scope and limits

- Public show websites only; no scraped private email addresses or personal data.
- Each entry identifies an official guest application page and a proposed editorial fit, **not** proof of an available slot or interest.
- Guest criteria must be confirmed for each client and rechecked before use. Applications may require the guest to submit personally; do not apply on anyone's behalf without platform permission.
- This sample does not assert recent episode activity, audience metrics, response rates, verified email deliverability, or acceptance.
- No paid subscriptions, APIs, or automation were used.

## Proposed service

A buyer can commission a scoped list by niche, guest profile, geography, publication recency, and contact requirements. Deliver CSV with show URL, evidence of relevant episodes, why it fits, public booking link, and confidence/exception notes. All commercial pricing and deadlines require buyer agreement before work. No guarantee of podcast bookings.
