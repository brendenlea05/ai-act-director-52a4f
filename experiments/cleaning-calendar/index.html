Recurring Cleaning Visits — Calendar Export

Free, experimental offline CSV-to-ICS helper for small cleaning businesses. It generates calendar events, not a CRM. The selected file is processed only in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

CSV columns: client,next_date,interval_days,start_time,duration_minutes,notes. Date must be YYYY-MM-DD; time HH:MM (24-hour). Set an interval of 7, 14 or 28 days for weekly, biweekly or four-week visits. Each row produces 12 occurrences and a one-day-before alarm request.

Before importing

  • Start with five non-sensitive clients. Check all dates and times against your real schedule.
  • Calendar apps may ignore imported alarms or recurring-event rules. Verify actual reminders in your chosen app.
  • Importing the same file twice may create duplicate events. This tool does not synchronize, cancel, or reschedule events.
  • Dates are floating local calendar times (not a fixed timezone). Test daylight-saving transitions and travel.
  • Keep a backup of your original CSV. Do not put private client details in public repositories or screenshots.
