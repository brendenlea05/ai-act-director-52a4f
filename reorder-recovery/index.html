Analyze an order history

Upload a CSV with the columns customer, email, order_date, sku, amount. One row per order line; include at least three distinct order dates per account–SKU. Add order_id if available. Date format YYYY-MM-DD.

How scores work

Signals are generated with transparent statistical rules, not AI predictions: a customer–SKU pair needs 3+ distinct purchase dates, the median interval between those dates becomes its normal cadence, and the latest gap must exceed both 1.35× that cadence and the cadence plus 7 days. This flags possible follow-ups only. Seasonality, contracts, stockouts, cancellations and customer preferences can explain a gap. The system does not estimate incremental revenue or send email.
